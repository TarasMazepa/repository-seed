# ToLooks

Exploratory ideas, investigative spikes, architectural inquiries, and potential refactors.

---

## Philosophy & Structure

- **Exploration over Premature Commitment**: Use [TOLOOKS.md](TOLOOKS.md) to capture ideas and insights mid-workflow without derailing current focus or ballooning PR scope.
- **Structured Items**:
  - `Idea`: What is the proposed concept, refactor, or experiment?
  - `Context`: Why does this matter? What problem, friction, or narrative does it address?
  - `Investigation`: Questions to explore, key trade-offs, or an actionable checklist.
- **Resolution Log**: When an item is addressed or decided against, mark it `[x]` and document a concise `Resolution` summary so the file preserves architectural reasoning over time.
